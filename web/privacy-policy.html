Privacy Policy

Entity: SebChris Mobility Pvt Ltd. ("RoAd RoBo's", "we", "us", "our")
Last Updated: August 2026

SebChris Mobility Pvt Ltd. operates the RoAd RoBo's mobile application, website, and related vehicle rental, taxi ride booking, package delivery, and emergency roadside assistance services (collectively, the "Services"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information with full transparency.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to deliver efficient, safe, and personalized mobility services:

  • Account Information: Name, phone number, email address, profile picture, and verified emergency contacts.
  • Identity & KYC Documents: Driver’s license and government ID copies (required strictly for vehicle rental verification and driver onboarding).
  • Transactional Information: Booking history, vehicle rental durations, delivery orders, payment receipts, and billing addresses.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, push notification tokens, and crash analytics logs.

📍 2. Location Information & Background Processing (Prominent Disclosure)

RoAd RoBo's collects precise and approximate location data from your mobile device to power essential mobility features.

Foreground Location: When the app is open and on screen, location is used to detect your current pickup point, calculate taxi fares, pinpoint vehicle rental hubs, and enable emergency SOS alerts.

Background Location (Even when closed or not in use): During an active ride, ongoing package delivery, driver dispatch shift, or emergency roadside assistance request, RoAd RoBo's accesses device location continuously in the background to:

  • Provide real-time turn-by-turn navigation and accurate ETAs to passengers and drivers.
  • Maintain live delivery parcel tracking updates for senders and recipients.
  • Enable emergency dispatchers and verified SOS contacts to locate you during roadside emergencies.

User Control: You can enable or disable location tracking anytime via your device’s system settings. Location data is encrypted in transit and is never sold to data brokers or used for third-party advertising.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your customer or driver account.
  • To dispatch rides, optimize routing, and facilitate package deliveries.
  • To verify driver licensing and prevent identity fraud on rental vehicles.
  • To send transactional updates, receipts, and security alerts.
  • To provide 24/7 emergency roadside support and dispatch assistance teams.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. We only share information with trusted service providers under strict data processing agreements:

  • Mapping & Routing: OpenStreetMap / Google Maps for location visualization and route calculation.
  • Payment Processors: RBI-compliant payment gateways (e.g., Razorpay) for secure transactions.
  • Cloud Infrastructure: Supabase and Firebase for encrypted data storage and push notifications.
  • Legal Obligations: When strictly required by applicable law or law enforcement agencies.

5. Data Retention & Security

We employ industry-standard encryption protocols (TLS/HTTPS in transit and AES-256 for sensitive database fields). We retain personal data only as long as necessary to provide Services and satisfy statutory accounting and regulatory requirements.

6. Account Deletion & User Rights

You have full control over your personal data, including the right to access, rectify, or permanently delete your account:

  • In-App Deletion: Go to Profile → Account Settings → Delete Account.
  • Web / Email Request: Email us directly at privacy@roadrobos.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request". All associated profile data, identity proofs, and saved locations will be purged within 30 days.

7. Children’s Privacy

Our Services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.
